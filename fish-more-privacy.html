Fish: More! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Fish: More! is a game published by Zep Interactive (“we”, “us”) for Android and web browsers. This policy explains what information is handled when you play it. Questions: zepinteractive@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no servers of ours, and the Android app does not use the internet. Your progress (coins, pearls, stars, upgrades, the fish you have found and your settings) is stored only on your device. Android may include it in your device's own backup to your Google account if you have backups turned on; that backup is handled by Google, not by us.

Playing on a web game portal

When you play Fish: More! on a website such as CrazyGames or Poki, that website runs the game page and may show ads and collect data under its own privacy policy. The game itself still keeps your progress only in your browser.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not keep any personal data, you can remove everything the game stores by uninstalling it or clearing its data in Android settings (or your browser's site data on the web). For any request, write to zepinteractive@gmail.com.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data (for example ads), we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.

Son güncelleme: 27 Ekim 2026

Topladığımız bilgiler

Hiçbir şey. Oyunda hesap, reklam, uygulama içi satın alma, analiz aracı veya bize ait bir sunucu yoktur; Android uygulaması internet kullanmaz. İlerlemen (paralar, inciler, yıldızlar, geliştirmeler, bulduğun balıklar ve ayarların) yalnızca cihazında saklanır. Yedekleme açıksa Android bunu cihazının Google hesabına yaptığı yedeğe ekleyebilir; bu yedeği biz değil Google yönetir.

Web oyun sitelerinde oynamak

Fish: More!'u CrazyGames veya Poki gibi bir sitede oynadığında oyun sayfasını o site çalıştırır; site kendi gizlilik politikasına göre reklam gösterebilir ve veri toplayabilir. Oyunun kendisi ilerlemeni yine yalnızca tarayıcında tutar.

Çocuklar
